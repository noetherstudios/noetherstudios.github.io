Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

CentCare is an app by Noether Studios for logging your cash and card expenses and setting a weekly limit. This policy explains what data it uses and what it does with it.

What we store

What you log in the app: your expenses (amount, category, note, date and how you paid), your cards (name, type, color and, if you want, the last 4 digits), your weekly limit and your settings. We never ask for your full card number, CVV, bank or passwords.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on your phone. CentCare has no accounts or servers, and your expenses never leave your device. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted.

Your bank's notifications (Android)

If you turn it on, CentCare uses Android's "Notification access" permission to read purchase alerts from the apps you choose (for example, your bank) and suggest the expense for you to confirm. It works like this:

- Only the apps you choose in Settings → Automatic bank expenses are read. - From those alerts only the amount, the merchant and the card's last digits are taken, and they are kept on your phone until you confirm or dismiss the expense. - For other apps, the text is not stored: only the app's name if it sent an alert with money, to suggest reading it. - None of this is sent to the internet, used for advertising or shared with anyone. - You can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings (Notification access).

CentCare's notifications

Weekly limit alerts and the cash reminder are scheduled on your phone. We don't send notifications from a server.

What we DON'T do

We don't sell your data, share it or use it for ads. CentCare doesn't collect personal data outside your phone.

Children

CentCare is not directed at children under 13.
