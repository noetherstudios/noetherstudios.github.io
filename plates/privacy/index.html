Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

What we store

What you log in the app: foods and portions, calorie and macro goals, weight, your profile for calculating goals (sex, age, height and activity level), workouts, routines and gyms. If you create an account, also your email.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on your phone first. If you sign in, it's backed up to the cloud with Supabase (servers in the United States) so you don't lose it if you switch phones. Each account can only see its own data.

Health data (Health Connect and Apple Health)

If you allow it, the app reads your active calories, resting or total calories and steps from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone), only to add them to your daily goal and show you your activity. It doesn't write anything there, doesn't store that data in the cloud, doesn't use it for advertising and doesn't share it with anyone. You can revoke the permission at any time from Health Connect settings or from the Health app on your iPhone.

Camera

The camera is used to scan barcodes and read nutrition labels. The photo is processed on your phone; photos aren't uploaded or saved.

Food search

To find a product, the app sends the barcode or the name you type to Open Food Facts, a public food database. None of your personal data is sent.

Payments

If you subscribe to Plates Pro, payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card. We use RevenueCat to know whether your subscription is active; for that it receives an identifier for your account and the app purchase history, nothing else.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We don't send notifications from a server.

What we DON'T do

We don't sell your data, don't show ads, don't use third-party trackers and don't share your information with anyone, except the services mentioned above that make the app work.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all your cloud data from the app: Goals → Account & backup → Delete my account. To delete the data on your phone, uninstall the app or choose "Delete from phone" when signing out. You can also request it by email.
